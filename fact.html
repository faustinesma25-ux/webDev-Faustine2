5 FACTS ABOUT ITALY

Global UNESCO Leader

Ties with China for the most UNESCO World Heritage Sites globally (55+).

The Colosseum

The largest ancient amphitheater ever built, holding up to 80,000 spectators for gladiatorial games.

Pizza's Birthplace

Modern pizza originated in Naples; Pizza Margherita matches the flag's colors.

Oldest University

University of Bologna (founded 1088) is the world's oldest operating university.

Sanctuary Cats

Roman law protects stray cats, with ruins doubled as cat shelters.
